The retrieval stage of the pipeline, tested the JudgeSaab way. Every bundled court case records the authorities the court actually cited — that is the ground truth, no hand-labeling. Given only the legal question, a retriever ranks a corpus of statutes, constitutional provisions, convention articles, and common-law doctrines (with distractors mixed in). The headline metric is precedent recall@k: of the authorities the court relied on, how many did the retriever surface? This is the retrieval analogue of the compression test's judgment fidelity. Runs entirely on your device, instantly — no model, no GPU.

The test is deterministic and instant: results update as you change any control. The leaderboard ranks retrievers by precedent recall@k — the share of the court's own cited authorities that the retriever surfaces.

How it works

  1. Query — take a case's legal question (optionally with facts) as the retrieval query.
  2. Corpus — rank a fixed pool of legal authorities (statutes, constitutional provisions, convention articles, doctrines) plus distractors.
  3. Ground truth — the authorities the court actually cited (from the case record) are the relevant documents. No annotation needed.
  4. Score — precedent recall@k, precision@k, hit rate, MRR, nDCG@k.
  5. Compare — swap the retriever and see which surfaces the precedents the court relied on.

Programmatic API on window.JudgeSaabRetrieval: runRetrieval(), compareRetrievers(), corpusInfo(). Part of the JudgeSaab pipeline-test family — one legal corpus, a ground-truth-anchored test per stage (compression, retrieval, reranking, …).
